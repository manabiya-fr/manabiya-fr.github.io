# Welcome to Manabiya

We provide [consulting](consulting.md) and [courses](courses/index.md) in software
engineering, mathematical optimization, operations research, and their applications. We
help organizations solve complex engineering and decision-making problems by combining
rigorous analysis with practical implementation.

Whether you need to optimize a production schedule, develop decision-support software,
or build intelligent real-time systems, we work closely with your team to transform
ideas into reliable solutions.

## Consulting

We help organizations tackle challenging optimization and engineering problems &mdash;
from resource allocation and scheduling to robotics and real-time decision-making.

If you're unsure whether your problem can be solved efficiently, we offer a [free proof
of concept](consulting.md) to demonstrate what is possible before you commit.

## Courses

Our [in-person courses](courses/index.md) bridge theory and practice in software
engineering, operations research, and mathematical optimization. Participants learn the
concepts, algorithms, and engineering techniques needed to solve real-world problems.
